## For each Note in your list
1. **Read the whole Note and its figures.** List its key ideas and standard terms.
2. **Illustrate every key idea and term.** Each one gets a figure or animation that makes the words concrete, on the Note's own data (assert its numbers in the script). Animate every process. Retraining models and running experiments to make figures never needs asking; heavy runs go to topgro, and Keras runs go on CPU when exact numbers matter.
3. **Ladder each section (§11):** plain idea and picture → standard term with its glossary ID → step-by-step mechanism on a worked example → formal version. Reorder or add short bridging sentences where needed. Do not add new facts without backing.
4. **Apply §9 and §10:** lists; IDs at a term's first use (look them up with `python3 tools/merge_glossary.py --id "<term>"`); replace informal stand-ins with the standard term, glossed simply; rewrite vague sentences into real mechanisms.
5. **Target:** "strong", meaning at most about 400 words per visual and at least 60% of sections with a figure. Aim higher where ideas are visual.
6. **Rebuild** with `tools/build.sh <folder>`; it must print "Built". Look at every new figure and frame grid, and at the PDF page where each sits.
